# Design Context — HolzpreisVergleich

## Target Audience
German homeowners researching firewood purchases. They arrive with a specific need: compare 2-5 supplier offers quickly and objectively. Mobile and desktop users equally. Non-technical; expect clarity, not cleverness.

## Use Cases
1. Enter 2-5 offers → get normalized €/fm and €/kWh comparison → pick the best deal
2. Look up heating values / wood species reference
3. Find local firewood suppliers
4. Check emissions and environmental info

## Brand Personality
**Sachlich · Neutral · Präzise** (Matter-of-fact · Neutral · Precise)

Not a lifestyle brand. A utility tool. Like a good calculator: invisible when it works, reliable always.

## Emotional Goal
**"Das war schnell & unkompliziert"** — Users should leave feeling the task took less effort than expected. No confusion, no second-guessing. In and out.

## Tone
Functional German utility. Closest physical analogy: the instruction card inside a tax form envelope — clear, unambiguous, no unnecessary decoration. Not cold or clinical, just efficient.

## Theme
Light mode. Users are on phones or laptops during daylight hours, comparing offers they received. Dark mode would feel wrong for a utility this practical.

## Anti-references
- NO lifestyle/fireplace-atmosphere imagery or dark cozy aesthetics
- NO "AI slop" dashboard aesthetics (gradient text, glowing cards, glassmorphism)
- NO marketing-website maximalism — this is a tool, not a landing page

## Fonts in Use
- Barlow (body, UI labels) — self-hosted WOFF2
- Barlow Condensed (headings, logo) — self-hosted WOFF2
- Fira Code (numbers, code-like values) — self-hosted WOFF2

## Color Tokens
- --pine: forest green (brand primary)
- --larch: warm amber (secondary / highlight)
- --paper: off-white background
- --ink variants: text hierarchy
